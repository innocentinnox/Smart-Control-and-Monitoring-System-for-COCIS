% ============================================================================
\section{Serial Command Reference}
\label{app:cli}
% ============================================================================

Commands marked \textbf{(admin)} need an admin session (\cref{sec:op-auth}); without one they
reply with a hint to log in. Every configuration change is written to the log with the admin's
card UID as \code{by=}.

{\small
\begin{longtable}{@{}p{0.38\linewidth}p{0.56\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
Command & Effect \\
\midrule
\endhead
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\emph{Monitoring}} \\
\code{status} & Time, uptime, schedule and whether open now (minutes to closing), lockdown,
  emergency and escort state, people inside, granted/denied/alert counts, storage state, rejected
  bus frames, RFID reader health, admin session, and a line per room. \\
\code{who} & People inside: UID, name, role, entry time. \\
\code{rooms} & Per room: link, occupancy, lamp level, mode, lux, energy used and saved (Wh). \\
\code{users} & Registered cards: UID, name, role, enabled, has PIN, inside. \\
\code{log tail [n]} & The newest \emph{n} log lines (default 10, at most 50). \\
\code{log verify [YYYYMMDD]} & Recompute the hash chain of a day's file (default today). \\
\code{log echo on|off} & Print every new log line live on the console. \\
\code{schedule} & Show the current access schedule. \\
\code{emergency} & Show the break-glass state. \\
\code{time} & Show the current time. \\
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\emph{Session}} \\
\code{login} & Start a login; then present the admin card at either reader. \\
\code{login <PIN>} & Second factor: the 4-digit PIN. \\
\code{logout} & End the admin session. \\
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\emph{Configuration (admin)}} \\
\code{schedule set HH:MM HH:MM [days]} & Set the access window; \emph{days} is \code{weekdays},
  \code{mon-sat}, \code{all} or a 7-letter mask such as \code{-MTWTF-} (Sunday first). Closing
  before opening means overnight. \\
\code{user add <UID> <name> <role> [PIN]} & Register a card (role \code{student}, \code{staff},
  \code{security} or \code{admin}; admins need a PIN). \\
\code{user del|enable|disable <UID>} & Remove, enable or disable a card. \\
\code{light <1|2> auto|on|off} & Override a room's lighting, or return it to automatic. \\
\code{lockdown on|off} & Only security and admin may pass while on. \\
\code{emergency clear} & End a break-glass emergency and close the doors. \\
\code{time set YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM} & Set the clock and the DS1307 RTC. \\
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\emph{Simulation}} \\
\code{tap <UID> in|out} & Scan any card at a reader, e.g.\ \code{tap C0FFEE99 in} for an
  unregistered card. \\
\code{node <1|2> offline|online} & Cut or restore a room's link to show offline detection. \\
\code{help} & List all commands. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
}
